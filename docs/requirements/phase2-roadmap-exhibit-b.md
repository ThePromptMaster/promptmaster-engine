## Purpose

This roadmap preserves the larger PromptMaster vision without making each future idea a current contractual obligation. Items should move into active development only after real-world validation, technical estimation, legal review where appropriate, and written scope approval.

## Roadmap Principle

Build the narrowest version that proves repeatable user value, while preserving an architecture that can grow.

## Stage 1 — Controlled Individual SaaS Beta

Phase 2 core: persistent projects, Book and Research workflows, resumable long-form work, editing, versions, evaluation, recommendations, and rationale.

Lightweight feedback and use-case capture.

Real-world testing with authors, researchers, consultants, recruiters, educators, and other frequent AI users.

Evidence of repeat usage, time saved, reduced context rebuilding, completed work, and willingness to pay.

## Stage 2 — Product Refinement and Additional Workflows

User-created action sets and workflow templates.

Business plan, consulting, recruiting, policy, course, software, and patent-related workflows where validated.

Advanced semantic continuity retrieval and citation management.

Additional export formats and improved onboarding education.

Model comparison, task-specific routing, and stronger cost optimization.

PromptMaster Cards or workflow packages based on proven use cases.

## Stage 3 — Team and Department Adoption

Organization accounts, shared workspaces, roles, reviewer flows, shared workflow libraries, and approved templates.

Basic team analytics focused on adoption, completion, bottlenecks, accepted recommendations, and evaluation improvement.

High-touch implementation, workflow mapping, training, support, and recurring SaaS subscription.

Department-specific use cases and bottom-up expansion from repeat users to team buyers.

## Stage 4 — Enterprise Governance

Enterprise SSO, advanced permissions, retention and deletion controls, audit exports, security review support, and private deployment options.

Policy and approval checkpoints for regulated or high-risk workflows.

Organization-level workflow and decision history.

Advanced administrative controls and customer-specific integrations through APIs.

## Stage 5 — Agent and Integration Layer

Documented external API for project state, artifacts, recommendations, evaluations, and authorized state changes.

MCP server and/or client capabilities if strategically justified.

Outside agents can read authorized project context, submit proposed work, return artifacts, receive evaluation, and request human approval.

Agent actions are constrained, recorded, and linked to objectives, workflow stages, and human decisions.

Model and agent orchestration remains subordinate to PromptMaster state and governance.

## Stage 6 — Organizational and Procedural Intelligence

Cross-project and organizational memory with explicit privacy and permission controls.

Reusable procedural knowledge about how approved work is performed.

Artifact lineage, decision ancestry, workflow ancestry, and organization-level learning.

Advanced analytics and recommendations derived from observed workflows rather than unsupported theoretical possibilities.

## Future Concepts Preserved for Evaluation

AI Council or structured multi-perspective review.

Advanced decision engine and decision-outcome tracking.

Workflow or Card marketplace.

Certification and education dashboards tied to the PromptMaster curriculum.

On-premises or customer-cloud deployment.

Local model support for proprietary data.

Advanced compliance packs and industry-specific governance.

Native mobile or desktop applications.

## Promotion Criteria

A roadmap item should enter active scope only when most of the following are true:

A defined user repeatedly experiences the underlying problem.

The existing product does not solve it adequately.

The proposed feature has a concrete success measure.

Users show repeat usage or willingness to pay.

The engineering dependency and cost are understood.

The feature strengthens rather than fragments the core architecture.

The change is documented for contract and patent review where relevant.

## Competitive Positioning Guardrail

PromptMaster should not compete with foundation-model providers on raw generation. Its defensible category is the management and governance of AI-assisted work: explicit project state, workflow progression, artifact continuity, actionable evaluation, explainable next-step guidance, and human control across models and future agents.
