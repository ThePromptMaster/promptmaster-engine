## SCREEN — Screening guide

**Version 2 — revised for D4, not approved. Version 1 approved under D3 (internal use) and unchanged in the record.** Basis: Wednesday 09:00 record (D4).

This guide is for evaluating candidates later. It does not assess anyone, and no candidates are identified.

### Requirement 1: Multisite manufacturing leadership

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Evidence | The candidate has held direct accountability for manufacturing operations across more than one site at the same time. They had authority over site results, not just a coordinating or advisory role. |
| Questions | Which sites did you lead at the same time, and what were you accountable for at each? How did you set standards and priorities across sites that differed? Describe a conflict between sites and how you resolved it. |
| Strong answer | Names the sites they were responsible for at once and the scope of their authority. Gives concrete mechanisms such as shared metrics, review cadence and how resources were allocated. Gives a specific example of a decision between sites and its outcome. |
| Weak answer | Single-site experience described as multisite. Influence without accountability. General statements about "alignment" with no mechanism or example. |

### Requirement 2: Experience improving plant throughput

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Evidence | The candidate led a change that measurably raised plant output. They can explain the baseline, what they did and the result. |
| Questions | Describe a throughput improvement you led. What was the constraint, what did you change, and how did you measure the result? What did you try that did not work? |
| Strong answer | Identifies the actual constraint. Gives a before-and-after measure. States their own role in the change as distinct from the team's. Says how the gain was sustained. |
| Weak answer | No baseline or measure. Credit claimed for results driven by others or by outside factors. Describes cost-cutting or headcount changes as throughput improvement without showing that output rose. |

### Requirement 3: Experience leading managers

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Evidence | The candidate has directly led people who themselves manage teams. |
| Questions | How many managers reported to you, and what did they run? How did you develop them or hold them accountable? Describe a time you had to replace or turn around a manager. |
| Strong answer | Gives a clear reporting structure. Shows how they hold managers accountable through outcomes, not by doing the managers' work. Gives a specific example of a manager decision, handled directly. |
| Weak answer | Only led individual contributors. Vague span of control. Avoids discussing difficult personnel decisions. |
