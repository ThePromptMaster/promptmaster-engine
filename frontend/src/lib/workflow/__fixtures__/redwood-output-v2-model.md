# West Entrance Closure: Frequently Asked Questions

**Which entrance is closed, and when?**
The west entrance at Redwood Member Center is closed on Tuesday 8:00 a.m. to noon. During that time, you will not be able to enter through the west entrance.

**Which entrance can I use?**
The main entrance stays open. Please use the main entrance while the west entrance is closed.

**Where is the accessible service desk?**
The accessible service desk is on the ground floor. It is open during the closure window, Tuesday 8:00 a.m. to noon.

**How do I get help?**
If you need assistance, ask at the main entrance. This is the usual way to ask for help at the center, and it does not change during the closure.

**Is the main entrance affected by the closure?**
No. The closure applies to the west entrance only, and the main entrance stays open throughout the closure window.
