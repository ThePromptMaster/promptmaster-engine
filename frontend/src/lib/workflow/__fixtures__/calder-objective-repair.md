# Objective and Success: VP of Operations Finalist Handoffs

## 1. Objective

In the user's words: *"Prepare the finalist handoffs for two fictional retained executive searches. Both clients are hiring a VP of Operations. Two consultants will take over tomorrow, one per client. Each should be able to open their own handoff and know exactly what to do for each finalist."*

The work is three records, kept separate:

- **Record 1.** The firm's reusable finalist checklist template. It sets no client-specific defaults (FIRM-01).
- **Record 2.** The Calder Motion handoff. It contains only Calder Motion's search information.
- **Record 3.** The Linden Works handoff. It contains only Linden Works' search information.

Each record cites source IDs so the consultant can check every instruction.

**Readers:** one consultant per client, taking over tomorrow, Saturday, November 28, 2026.

- Calder Motion consultant: Owner: to confirm.
- Linden Works consultant: Owner: to confirm.

The material names neither consultant.

## 2. What success looks like for a consultant reading cold

Each entry is labelled **Supplied** when it comes directly from a source. It is labelled **Reasoned** when it is drawn from a source but not stated in it.

The Calder Motion terms come from two sources:

- **CAL-01 (Morgan Lee only).** Calder Motion conducts its own reference calls. The consultant sends the reference contact sheet and does not call references. These terms now govern Morgan Lee alone.
- **CAL-RIV (Rivera exception, 27 Nov).** Calder Motion authorized a one-time exception for Alex Rivera only. Under it:
  - The consultant conducts exactly one reference call and sends Calder a written summary.
  - Rivera's reference contact sheet stays internal and is not sent to Calder Motion.
  - For Rivera, no reference call has been made and no contact sheet or summary has been sent.

**SUPERSEDED: CAL-01 as applied to Alex Rivera.** CAL-01 covered both Calder finalists. On 27 Nov, CAL-RIV replaced it for Alex Rivera only. Approver: Calder Motion.

### C. Template (Record 1)

The template succeeds when it does three things:

- It carries the three FIRM-01 fields for each finalist: who contacts references, what the consultant sends to the client, and the next action.
- It leaves each field to be filled from the engagement's own terms.
- It contains no Calder Motion or Linden Works default, so a future search can use it unchanged.

## 3. Main risk: crossed instructions

The two engagements' rules are close to opposite. The Rivera exception also creates a split inside Calder Motion:

| Point | Calder Motion: Morgan Lee | Calder Motion: Alex Rivera | Linden Works: Alex Chen, Morgan Patel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who calls references | Client (CAL-01, Lee only) | Consultant, exactly one call (CAL-RIV) | Consultant, two calls each (LIN-01) |
| What is sent | Reference contact sheet (CAL-01, Lee only) | Written summary; contact sheet withheld (CAL-RIV) | Written summary, which must contain **no** phone numbers or email addresses (LIN-01) |

**What a mix-up would mean (Reasoned):**

- **Linden's practice applied to Morgan Lee:** calling references goes against CAL-01's terms for Lee.
- **Calder's practice for Lee applied to Linden:** sending a contact sheet would expose the reference phone numbers and email addresses that LIN-01 keeps internal.
- **Calder's practice for Lee applied to Alex Rivera:** sending his contact sheet breaks CAL-RIV.
- **The Rivera exception applied to Morgan Lee:** calling Lee's references and withholding his sheet breaks CAL-01's terms for Lee. The exception covers Alex Rivera only.
- **Linden's call count applied to Alex Rivera:** a second call exceeds the one call that CAL-RIV authorizes.

**Name confusion adds to the risk.** First names repeat across the two clients:

- Alex Rivera (Calder) and Alex Chen (Linden). Both now get a consultant call and a written summary, so the difference that matters is the number of calls: one for Rivera, two for Chen.
- Morgan Lee (Calder) and Morgan Patel (Linden).

If a finalist is named by first name only, or a document is filed under the wrong client, the wrong action or the wrong material goes out.

**Controls the records must carry (Reasoned):**

- Each record shows the client name at the top.
- Each finalist is identified by full name only.
- Each record contains no finalist or terms from the other client.
- Each record states its prohibited action or content in its own terms section.
- The Calder record shows the Rivera exception on Alex Rivera's row and marks it as applying to him only.
